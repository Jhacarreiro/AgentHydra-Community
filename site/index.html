The Hub

A home server that knows what every PC is doing.

Run the Hub in Docker on a machine that stays on: a mini PC, a NAS, an old laptop. Each PC pairs with it once and keeps its chats flowing in. Open the Hub from any PC, or from your phone, and see everything that is working or waiting on you.

  • Pair a PC with one click. A small tray app on Windows, macOS or Linux pairs through a page behind your own sign-in, starts at login, and shows green when the PC is reporting.
  • A token per PC. Each PC gets its own credential, so you can revoke a lost laptop without touching the others.
  • Continue on the right machine. Reply to a chat from the Hub and the turn runs on its own PC, with its files, its account and its approval prompts.
  • Updates itself. The Hub container keeps itself current, and the tray apps take only builds signed with your key.
ChatGPT

The conversations you have in ChatGPT, beside the ones in your terminal.

Half the planning happens in ChatGPT and the work happens in Claude Code or Codex. AgentHydra puts both in the same list, so the idea and the branch that came out of it sit next to each other.

  • Your real account. The Hub keeps a Chrome window signed in to your own ChatGPT, on your server. No API key, no export to upload.
  • Read, search, reply. Conversations show up with their titles and projects, and you can answer from the same composer you use for code.
  • Hand work across. Turn a ChatGPT thread into a task for Claude Code or Codex on any paired PC, or pack a repository into a prompt for ChatGPT.
  • Off until you turn it on. Nothing talks to ChatGPT unless you enable it and sign in yourself.

Install

Ten minutes from download to every chat in one place.

STEP 1 · THE WINDOW

Install on your PC

One line in PowerShell, no administrator. The download is checked against its SHA-256 before it unpacks.

irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Jhacarreiro/AgentHydra-Community/main/install.ps1 | iex
STEP 2 · OPTIONAL

Start a Hub

On any machine with Docker. It keeps itself up to date from then on.

docker compose -f deploy/docker/compose.yaml up -d --build
STEP 3 · OPTIONAL

Pair your PCs

Run the tray app on each PC and press Connect. Turn on ChatGPT in the Hub's settings and sign in once.
